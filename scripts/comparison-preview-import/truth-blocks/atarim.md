ANGLE: Atarim grew out of the WordPress-agency world. In July 2026 it is a visual
collaboration platform for agencies, freelancers, and creative teams. It works on any site
by URL, by Chrome extension, or by a deep native WordPress plugin. The headline product is
now the InnerCircle, a team of six AI agents that review any page. Verified as such July
2026 (atarim.io, atarim.io/pricing, atarim.io/product/visual-collaboration).

## Strengths
- AI actually checks the site. Six InnerCircle agents (Pixel design, Navi UX and accessibility, Index SEO and GEO, Claro clarity, Lexi copy, Glitch QA and bugs) run an AI review that scans a page for issues in seconds, with full page context (layout, spacing, copy, visuals, structure) (atarim.io, atarim.io/help/integrations/install-wordpress-plugin).
- Clients and stakeholders are unlimited and free on every plan. They comment with no account through Guest Mode, a shared link, or ?collab=true on any URL (atarim.io/pricing, atarim.io/help/integrations/install-wordpress-plugin).
- Three ways to review one live page. Drop any public URL, use the Chrome extension on any site, or install the WordPress plugin for native front-end and WP Admin review on live, staging, and login-protected sites (atarim.io/product/visual-collaboration, atarim.io/help/integrations/install-wordpress-plugin).
- Auto capture on every task: screenshot, browser type, screen resolution, and the exact URL, plus element data (atarim.io/help/getting-started/introduction-to-atarim-overview-and-benefits).
- Real memory concept. Atarim AI holds Global, Project, and User context layers, so brand standards, workflows, and business details persist as background reference and are not re-entered each time (atarim.io/help/atarim-ai/how-to-guide-ai-feedback-using-global-project-and-page-context).
- Native two-way sync. Comments post on both sides and statuses sync when possible, across Jira, Asana, ClickUp, Trello, Monday.com, Basecamp, Teamwork, and Slack (atarim.io/product/workflow-and-integrations).

## Limits
- AI is admin and team only. Clients and guests never see AI features or AI tasks. They get a plain comment interface (atarim.io/help/integrations/install-wordpress-plugin).
- AI usage is metered by monthly AI credits: 200 one time on Free, 2000 on Pro, 5000 on Business (atarim.io/pricing).
- Priced per seat with hard project caps: 2 projects on Free, 5 on Pro, 10 on Business (atarim.io/pricing).
- Seats are capped low on paid tiers: Pro maxes at 5 seats, Business at 100. Workspaces are 1 / 1 / 2 (atarim.io/pricing).
- No screen or video recording of feedback found. Capture is auto screenshots plus metadata (checked July 2026).

## Pricing (verified July 2026, atarim.io/pricing)
- Priced per seat. Annual billing gives 2 months free. Clients, guests, and stakeholders are unlimited on every plan.
- Free: $0. 200 AI credits one time, 2 active projects, 1 workspace, 1 seat. No card needed.
- Pro: $25/seat per month billed yearly ($29/seat monthly). 2000 AI credits, 5 active projects, 1 workspace, 1 seat up to 5.
- Business: $35/seat per month billed yearly ($42/seat monthly). 5000 AI credits, 10 active projects, 2 workspaces, 1 seat up to 100, adds Clarity Test and AI Discovery Sessions.
- Enterprise: custom. Custom AI credits, projects, and workspaces, AI Chat, SOC-2, dedicated success manager and team training.
- No page-view caps advertised. Site scope is set by the active-project cap, not by views.

Drop any public URL, use the Chrome extension on any site, or install the WordPress plugin for native front-end and WP Admin review on live, staging, and login-protected sites.
