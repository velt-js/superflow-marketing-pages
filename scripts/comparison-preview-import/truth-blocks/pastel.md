# Truth block: Pastel (verified July 2026, from usepastel.com)

Researched only from the vendor's own site (usepastel.com, usepastel.com/plans,
usepastel.com/features, usepastel.com/how-it-works, usepastel.com/website-revisions,
and help.usepastel.com). Rules: facts from the vendor's own pages only, dated.
Unverified stays a dash. Aggregator review sites rejected. Absence of evidence is
"not yet verified", never a "no". Pastel is a fast website-markup tool for client
feedback rounds. It also reviews PDFs, images, and video files.

## Strengths
- Zero install on the live site. No code snippet and no browser extension. You paste a URL, Pastel makes a "canvas", and you share one link (usepastel.com/website-revisions, usepastel.com/how-it-works)
- The canvas stays current. Pastel serves your live site through its own proxy, so when you change the site the canvas updates automatically. It is not a static copy or snapshot (help.usepastel.com/en/articles/1996011)
- Formal approval flow. The "Finish Review" button lets a client or teammate approve or request changes on a canvas. Approvals are tied to a version and reset when a new version is made. Available on all plans including Free (help.usepastel.com/en/articles/5899720)
- Guests need no account and no login. Unlimited guest reviewers on every plan. They click and type to comment (usepastel.com/plans, help.usepastel.com/en/articles/6443110)
- Every comment auto-captures a screenshot plus browser type and screen size (help.usepastel.com/en/articles/6443110)

## Limits
- Nothing checks the site on its own. A person finds every issue, then pins a comment. No AI features found on the vendor site (checked July 2026)
- No memory concept in product. Each canvas starts blank, and approvals reset on each new version (checked July 2026)
- Proxy-served, not the real page in place. Your site is routed through Pastel's proxy to render the canvas (help.usepastel.com/en/articles/1996011)
- PM-tool exports (Trello, Monday.com, Asana, Jira, custom webhook, Zapier) are gated to the Team plan ($119/mo) (usepastel.com/plans, help.usepastel.com/en/articles/2098571)
- Active-canvas caps on lower tiers: Free 1, Pro 3. Video storage caps per tier (usepastel.com/plans)

## Open dashes (re-verify before rendering as fact)

## Pricing (verified July 2026, usepastel.com/plans)
- Free Forever $0/mo: 1 user, 1 active canvas, unlimited guest reviewers, private comments, unlimited versions per canvas, file attachments, user mentions, export to CSV, 2GB video storage
- Pro $35/mo: everything in Free, 2 users, 3 active canvases, export to PDF, 100GB video storage
- Team $119/mo: everything in Pro, starts at 5 users, $24/user/mo for more, unlimited active canvases, export to Trello/Asana/Jira/etc., export to webhook or Zapier, 500GB video storage
- Enterprise $450/mo: everything in Team, starts at 10 users, $45/user/mo for more, SAML SSO, SOC 2 report, custom terms, dedicated account rep
- Annual billing saves 17%. All paid plans get a 14-day free trial, no credit card. Guest reviewers are unlimited on all plans

## Migration / import tooling
- Canvas creation by URL, or by uploading images, PDFs, emails, and video files (help.usepastel.com/en/articles/2098626)
- CSV export of comments on all plans; PDF export on Pro and up (usepastel.com/plans)
- No dedicated importer from rival feedback tools found on the vendor site (checked July 2026)
