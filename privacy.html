6. WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU CONNECT SUPERFLOW TO AN AI ASSISTANT?

In Short: If you connect SuperFlow to an AI assistant, the assistant can read and change your SuperFlow data only as you allow.

SuperFlow offers a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. It lets you connect your SuperFlow account to AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT and Grok, so you can ask the assistant to read or work with your SuperFlow data.

  • You choose to connect. Nothing is shared until you add SuperFlow in the assistant and sign in. You sign in with OAuth and approve access, or use an API key you create.
  • What the assistant can see. The assistant sees only what your SuperFlow account can see, in the one workspace you pick. It can read data such as projects, pages, reviews and comments, and can make changes you ask for.
  • Changes need your confirmation. Before an action changes data, sends an email or spends AI credits, the assistant shows a preview and asks you to confirm.
  • What we receive. We receive the requests the assistant makes to SuperFlow and the inputs it sends with them. We do not receive your full conversation with the assistant.
  • What the assistant's provider receives. Data we return goes to the assistant and its provider, such as Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI. Their own privacy policies cover how they handle it, not this policy.
  • Logs. We log each request (which action, when, which account and workspace) to keep the service secure and to help with support.
  • Disconnect anytime. You can remove SuperFlow from the assistant, revoke access in your SuperFlow settings, or delete the API key. After that, the assistant can no longer reach your data.
